ℹ️ About AgriSense AI

Production-grade decision-support system for agricultural crop suitability recommendation.

🎯 Project Objectives

AgriSense AI bridges modern machine learning with agricultural science by analyzing soil nutrients (N, P, K, pH), environmental factors (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall, Sunlight), and regional farm parameters to recommend the most suitable crop out of 20 distinct species.

🛠️ Technology Stack

  • Language & Runtime: Python 3.11+
  • Machine Learning: Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Random Forest, Extra Trees, Stacking Classifier
  • Backend Framework: FastAPI, Pydantic v2, Uvicorn
  • Database & ORM: SQLite, SQLAlchemy ORM
  • Frontend: Modern HTML5, CSS3 Glassmorphism, Vanilla JavaScript, Plotly.js
  • Testing & DevOps: Pytest, Docker, Docker Compose
Standard Academic & Operational Disclaimer:
AgriSense AI is an educational and decision-support prototype. It does not guarantee specific crop yield or economic returns. Always consult local agricultural extension officers or certified agronomists before undertaking major farming decisions.
